\section*{Declaration of generative-AI use}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Declaration of generative-AI use}

This dissertation was written with Claude Code (Anthropic,
\url{https://claude.com/claude-code}), using Claude models (Opus~4.x,
Sonnet~4.x/5, Fable~5.1) during 2026, as a coding and writing assistant
under the author's direction: implementing GHX and the analysis scripts,
running and monitoring the campaigns, drafting and editing this text, and
simulating reviewer feedback. Experimental design, the evidence-whitelist
and exclusion rulings, the interpretation of results and every final
wording decision are the author's; every number in the text carries a
ledger anchor and, where classed \cls{A}, is reproduced by a script in the
repository. The LLMs inside the system under study --- the solver and the
four meta-roles, DeepSeek models served through a LiteLLM gateway --- are
the object of the experiments (Chapters~\ref{ch:baseline}
and~\ref{ch:design}), not part of this declaration.

\section*{Code and data access}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Code and data access}

The vendored system, this thesis's own code, the recompute scripts
and the thesis sources live in one repository,
\url{https://github.com/GaryGAO2003/GraphHarnessX} (branch
\texttt{main}, tagged \texttt{thesis-2026-09} at the point of
submission). Recompute scripts are under \texttt{experiments/analysis/};
thesis sources under \texttt{experiments/docs/thesis/}; the fact ledger
is Appendix~\ref{app:ledger}; the pre-specification record behind
\S\ref{sec:efficacy-protocol} is \texttt{docs/provenance/}. The
repository is private; the examiners are granted access on request to
the author. The six whitelisted run archives are large trajectory
stores, kept outside the repository and available from the author on
request.
