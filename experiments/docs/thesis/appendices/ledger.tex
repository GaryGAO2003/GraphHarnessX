\chapter{Fact Ledger}
\label{app:ledger}
% =========================================================================

Every number in the body of this thesis carries an \F{} anchor into this
ledger; the abstract restates body figures without anchors. Each
row states the fact, its value, the \emph{sample and scope} it was computed
over, and its source class:

\begin{description}[leftmargin=3em,style=nextline]
  \item[\textnormal{\cls{A}}] \textbf{Recomputable.} A named script
        regenerates the number from raw run artifacts. May be stated
        directly in the body.
  \item[\textnormal{\cls{B}}] \textbf{Recorded.} Observed and logged, but
        not regenerable from a script --- typically an operational event.
        Must be written as ``recorded at\ldots'' at its point of use.
  \item[\textnormal{\cls{C}}] \textbf{Judgment.} An interpretation. Must be
        labelled as judgment at its point of use.
\end{description}

Two rules govern the ledger and are stated here because they constrain the
whole document. \emph{Sample and scope must appear in the same paragraph as
the number}, so that no figure can be quoted away from the population it
was computed over. And \emph{no number enters the thesis before its ledger
row and its recompute script both exist} --- a rule this project adopted
after discovering that several of its own early headline figures could not
be regenerated.

Script paths are relative to \texttt{experiments/analysis/} unless given in
full. Where a row's scope was narrowed by the whitelist ruling
(\S\ref{sec:scope}), the row states the narrowed scope and the superseded
reading appears in the retraction registry rather than here. \textbf{F17},
\textbf{F27} and \textbf{F28}
are supporting or archival rows: measured and recorded, cross-checking other rows or reporting a
probe the body does not need, and not meant for standalone citation.

\item[\textbf{F9c}\quad Why F9a and F9b are not an arm comparison.]
Four compounding defects. \emph{(i)} the arms' 22 shipped candidates are
22 \emph{different} edits, not one treatment twice. \emph{(ii)} base
pool $25.2\%$ against $34.2\%$, so equal hit rates would already give
unequal lift. \emph{(iii)} five gates against six, the sixth testing
whether a cited graph node exists --- \emph{unrelated to predictive
accuracy} --- thinning the sample. \emph{(iv)} the graph arm moves
$0.78 \to 1.23$, $n: 9 \to 2$, across readings. \emph{(v)} graph-arm core/halo labels cover a broader prediction
population than the declared unlock lists and omit registry-tool touches
\F{64}. \emph{Conclusion:} the localization face is
\textbf{not adjudicable}. \emph{Class:} \cls{A} for the components,
\cls{C} for the conclusion.

\item[\textbf{F9d}\quad Uncertainty on the lift estimates.]
Cluster bootstrap with the \emph{candidate} as resampling unit (20{,}000
draws; predictions inside one candidate share an edit, a round and a base
pool). No-graph $1.99$, $95\%$ CI $[1.47, 2.54]$ --- excludes $1.0$
(design effect $0.93$). Graph $0.78$, $95\%$ CI $[0.34, 1.23]$ ---
\textbf{includes $1.0$, indistinguishable from chance} (design effect
$1.50$). The $n=2$ window reading is \textbf{refused an interval}: three
possible resamples, design effect $0.10$, below the script's minimum
cluster count. \emph{Minimum detectable lift} (one-sided exact binomial,
$\alpha = 0.05$, each arm against its own base pool): no-graph $1.51$,
graph $1.37$ uncorrected and $\approx 1.46$ at the measured design effect.
\emph{Reading:} the graph arm could carry a genuine $+40\%$ localization
improvement and this design would not see it. \emph{Class:} \cls{A},
\texttt{audit\_lift\_uncertainty.py}, with a self-check gate that
reproduces the point estimates from the independent script before printing
an interval.

\item[\textbf{F34}\quad Efficacy verdict on the pre-specified test.]
Prospective batch (7 clean tasks $\times 10 \times 2$, same window):
\textbf{$+1/-4$, sign test $p = 0.97$} (parent 29/70, applied 21/70).
Pooling every clean same-window pair, \textbf{174 pairs: parent 75/174
against applied 72/174} (Fisher one-sided $0.67$). Mechanism endpoint
also flat: confident-wrong among failures 51/99 against 54/102. A stopping
computation showed the second batch could not reverse the result, so it was
cancelled. \textbf{Verdict: no score improvement, with a negative point estimate}
(parent 29/70 against applied 21/70 prospectively, 75/174 against 72/174
pooled) --- and the preceding four apparent wins were window noise, each
killed by a different instrument. \emph{Resolution law for this bed:} a
single task's base rate swings $\pm 50$ points on a half-hour scale, and a
single-window $\Delta \le 5$ is unreadable. \emph{Sample:} 174 pairs, the
two leakage-cluster tasks excluded. \emph{Record:} rules written in the
working notes before launch, committed together with the verdict; see
\S\ref{sec:efficacy-protocol} and \texttt{docs/provenance/}. \emph{Class:} \cls{A},
\texttt{proof\_stats.py}.

\emph{Class:} \cls{B}, recorded candidate dossier

\section*{C.2\quad The retraction registry}
% -------------------------------------------------------------------------

Appended to the ledger rather than hidden in it. It records the claims this
project withdrew whose replacement a reader of the surviving numbers would
otherwise have to reconstruct; the measurement errors behind several of
them are registered separately, with their rules, in
Appendix~\ref{app:deviations}. \textbf{Nothing in this registry is
re-cited anywhere in this thesis.}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=0pt,labelindent=0pt,labelsep=0pt,style=nextline,font=\normalfont,itemsep=5pt]

\item[``A harness ceiling of 3 points''] $\to$ F16 and F17 presented
together; the 3 is a lower bound on one queue.

\item[``Halved cost is evidence for the graph''] $\to$ F13: portable
engineering.

\item[``Net expensive graph layer''] $\to$ \textbf{withdrawn}. It was
measured on campaigns the whitelist excludes; on the admissible campaigns
the task-side direction is the opposite, with the graph arm cheaper per
fresh evaluation on all three seeds; and the meta layer, where the graph
adds work, has no admissible cost record. \emph{The correct entry is
blank, not negative.}

\item[``The official loop cannot prescribe cause-aware repairs''] $\to$
F38: the archaeology falsifies it; all three ancestors were Critic-accepted
and the verify family appeared on a zero-graph arm at $k{=}1$.

\item[``$+7/+5/+4/+6$ shows the three-drug pack working''] $\to$ F34: four
consecutive windows of noise, each executed by a different instrument.

\item[``The $+7$ was drug effect''] $\to$ F36: the runs where it fired
scored 0/5, and a same-window re-test gave 6/10 against 6/10.

\item[``Better aim will produce gain''] $\to$ F40(iv): hits produce no
feedback, so aiming gains cannot be realized.

\item[The localization lift, in four superseded forms] $\to$ F9b--F9d.
``Graph-arm lift $0.64$ at $n{=}8$'' became $0.78$ at $n{=}9$ once the
campaign was completed to sixteen rounds; ``$1.99$ against $0.64$ shows
localization did not improve'' is void because the two numbers
\textbf{cannot be subtracted}; ``the graph arm's $0.78$ is a decline'' is
void because its $95\%$ interval includes $1.0$, the correct word being
\emph{unmeasured} with a minimum detectable lift near $1.46$; and an
$n{=}2$ window reading is refused an interval as a degenerate bootstrap.
The surviving statement is that this bed cannot adjudicate the
localization face.

\item[Five pre-specified criteria, as a section] $\to$ \textbf{section
removed}. What is retained: F34's prospective efficacy batch remains the
thesis's one confirmatory test, with its stopping rule; every other
$p$-value is labelled exploratory.

\item[``The graph tightened cross-seed variance''] $\to$ does not hold and
does not enter the thesis. The plateau SD ratio looks supportive, but the
round-0 variance ratio --- before the graph has acted --- is more extreme
still, so the difference predates the treatment.

\item[``Fix the volatile family and the bed is clean''] $\to$ F54: the 21
stable tasks have zero variance and the 79 that can move are the ones that
move by themselves; no subset is both reliable and informative.

\item[``H1 is falsified'', unqualified] $\to$ H1's original endpoint is
the localization face, and that face is \emph{unmeasurable} here.
Replacement: \textbf{H1 is not supported on its declared substitute
endpoints}
--- archaeology (strongest, and independent of any lift number), efficacy,
and the outcome face.

\item[``The evidence column has a 75\% false-negative rate'',
      with the implication that those calls mattered] $\to$ F2/F3 restated
in three tiers: \emph{(i)} construction --- it checks only a
$\ge 20$-character verbatim overlap with the immediately following message;
\emph{(ii)} blindness on this bed --- median answer length 9, p90 14, below
the column's own threshold, so short-fact use is unobservable in principle;
\emph{(iii)} rate --- of 559 calls whose output contains a passing round's
final answer, 419 ($75.0\%$) are graded not referenced, $96.7\%$ of them
with answers below the threshold. \textbf{Tier (iii) is a property of the
column's output only}: a call on the success path holding the answer is
not a call that caused the pass. The conclusion rests on (i) and (ii).

\item[``The one ratchet click is the only above-band move''] $\to$ F48:
eleven above-band moves across the six campaigns, six of them in rounds
where nothing shipped. What survives is narrower and better supported ---
the positive control is the only above-band move whose mechanism the record
can read.

\end{description}
