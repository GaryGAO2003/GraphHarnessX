% -------------------------------------------------------------------------
\section{The readout proposition and its scope}
\label{sec:proposition}
% -------------------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection*{The proposition}

Self-evolution is usually described as variation plus selection. This
thesis argues it has a third term:

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Self-evolution $=$ variation $+$ selection $+$ readout.} When the
effects being selected are small relative to variation in the score
readout, score-based retention may mis-rank edits. The cumulative-gain
statement is a hypothesis motivated by this case, not a dynamic estimated
by these campaigns.
\end{quote}

It is a proposition rather than a theorem: as stated in words it restates
power analysis, and what makes it non-trivial is the dynamics argument
below, which a single-test power calculation does not supply.

The claim is made for this bed and this system class, under three
conditions held fixed here: the activation regime (which candidates may
ship, and when), the pairing scheme (same-configuration windows as the
readout reference), and the admission gates. Changing any one changes the
flip rate the proposition is read against, and the claim must then be
re-established. The field-level
implication --- that every surveyed loop selects on aggregate score
(\S\ref{sec:self-evolving}) and is therefore exposed to the same
arithmetic on any bed with a comparable flip rate --- is an \emph{inference}
and is flagged as one.  An independent, contemporaneous
critique of how harness evolution is evaluated reaches a convergent
conclusion under a different evaluation design~\cite{rethink-harness-eval}.

\subsection*{Formal spine}

Quantitative genetics already has the equation. The response to selection
is given by the breeder's equation~\cite{falconer-mackay},
\[
  R \;=\; h^2 S ,
\]
gain per generation equals heritability times the selection differential.
When selection acts on a \emph{measured} phenotype rather than the true
one, $h^2$ is attenuated by the measurement's reliability --- classical
regression dilution~\cite{hutcheon-dilution}:
\[
  h^2_{\text{realized}} \;=\; h^2_{\text{true}}\,\rho ,
  \qquad
  \rho \;=\; \frac{\sigma^2_{\delta}}{\sigma^2_{\delta}+\sigma^2_{\varepsilon}} .
\]

The reliability form is retained here as a conditional analogy. These
campaigns do not estimate the effect-size variance, measurement-error
variance, or retention dynamics needed to instantiate $\rho$; in
particular, the $3.70$ in \S\ref{sec:power} is the sample SD of twenty
paired same-configuration score changes, not $\sigma_\varepsilon$.

\paragraph{The dynamics claim.} On this bed and system class, expected
cumulative gain can be non-positive when selection mis-ranks small
effects. That is a hypothesis consistent with this case; no empirical
$\rho$, false-kill law, or variation-quality-independent result is
claimed.

\paragraph{Boundary condition.} The breeder's equation governs the
\emph{mean} response and is silent on the tail. The guard event is an
observed positive score movement with a legible mechanism proxy, not an
identified causal tail effect. The proposition retains the theoretical
mean--tail boundary without assigning that event efficacy.

\paragraph{Six positive slopes.} All six campaigns climb, and
\S\ref{sec:trend} accounts for the slopes without crediting any edit; the
residual climb is consistent with a cumulative real gain of that size and
with its absence \F{47}. The proposition does not say cumulative gain is
absent, only that the available score readout may not reliably rank small effects edit by edit; the
post-round clamp, the one score-keyed retention action here, fired five
times, three of them on drops inside the band, and once reverted the
processor whose round had cut starvation exits from 21 to 2 \F{66}\F{49}.

\subsection*{The mechanism-consistent event that motivates the test}

The budget-starvation event (\S\ref{sec:one-repair}) is a post-hoc,
mechanism-consistent observation: \texttt{budget\_exceeded} exits fell from
35 to 4, twelve of the sixteen newly passing tasks had exited after budget exhaustion
in the preceding round, and the $+9$ cleared the same-configuration band
\F{39}. A processor, a prompt and a tool shipped together, with no
same-window counterfactual; the event neither isolates the processor's
efficacy nor establishes assay sensitivity, and the processor left the
configuration four rounds later.

\subsection*{Alternative capability-limit account}

Each of the null results of \S\ref{sec:clinic} is also consistent with an
alternative to the readout proposition: \textbf{the loop's action space may
not reach the relevant targets.}
This capability-limit account is one Class-C latent failure explanation
\F{40}. If candidate targets are outside the available action space, it can explain a flat outcome axis without identifying the readout
as the cause.

On the outcome axis the two are observationally equivalent. Rung R3 was
exercised by hand
\F{34}\F{36} and never wired into retention, so no experiment here
separates them.

The evidence does not separate the rivals. The mechanism-consistent guard
event's processor left its campaign four rounds later, and concurrent ships
and the absent counterfactual prevent causal attribution \F{39}. Repeated
independent proposals show recurring diagnoses \F{38},
but do not establish efficacy. Together these observations leave readout
and actuator jointly unresolved, neither a capability wall nor a readout
gap being excluded; the wrong-closure family --- failures that end in a
normal exit with a wrong answer --- still contains 18 of the 49 swing tasks
\F{31}.

\paragraph{The discriminating experiment.} Attach the R3 readout to
retention and re-fly with intervention-specific counterfactuals. Persistent
drift would still require separating actuator limits, treatment efficacy,
and retention error; it would not identify one bottleneck by itself.

\section{Governance: necessary, not sufficient}
\label{sec:governance}
% -------------------------------------------------------------------------

The acceptance gates fire, within their remit; their refusal correctness is unverified. A retrospective application of
the criterion flags 6 of 22 selected shipped tools; because the graph subset
consists of post-gate survivors, this describes criterion outcomes only within
the selected sample and does not estimate the gate's false-refusal rate \F{71}.
Eight structural refusals are on the record in the whitelist graph seed-1
campaign, one campaign only \F{11}. No refused candidate shipped as filed;
two refused tools later landed through re-proposed cards \F{71}.

Their remit includes execution-record support, but not the source provenance of a capability or datum. Under a coverage directive --- treat
every task, never abstain --- the loop engineered the bed's own leak routes
into a benchmark-lookup cheat tool, and that tool passed the Critic and
\emph{every} landing gate \F{37}. Nothing in the ladder asks where a
capability came from.

The bed-side leakage and loop-side file access expose the same
governance gap from two sides. The concrete file-access incident and absent
bed protections are recorded in \S\ref{sec:threats}.

\section{Threats to validity}
\label{sec:threats}
% -------------------------------------------------------------------------

\paragraph{Attribution is arm-level.} The score dashboards supply no
common-endpoint arm contrast (Table~\ref{tab:results}). No single-factor attribution of score
effects is claimed \F{9c}. The per-switch entries of the profit-and-loss
account establish construction or capability facts, not outcome effects;
\S\ref{sec:future} specifies experiments needed to estimate the latter.

\paragraph{The localization metric is post-hoc.} It is defined over
predictions that were written before the outcome was known but scored
under a rule fixed afterwards. This is stated at every point of use.

\paragraph{Single bed, single model tier.} Three seeds per arm show that
the observed variation replicates within this measured
bed--solver--harness--judge--environment setup \F{44}\F{45}; generalization
beyond it is untested.

\paragraph{The noise band is transferred across arms.} The band and the
paired-change SD are measured on the no-graph arms' same-config windows only
\F{9e}; the graph arm has no ship-free whole-bed window
(Appendix~\ref{app:cost}), so every graph-arm reading against the band
assumes the same noise.

\paragraph{Measurement policies differ between the arms.} Every cross-arm
reading is recomputed on the 100-task bed from \texttt{task\_history}
rather than taken from \texttt{curves.json} \F{46}. The common
denominator does not make the measurement policies equal: graph-arm
dashboards include selectively carried outcomes, so no common
contemporaneous score contrast is claimed.

\paragraph{Leak-route contamination.} Thirteen of the 49 swing tasks in
the whitelist graph seed-1 campaign are leak-route \F{31}. Repeated exposure to the same fixed bed gives both arms
a route to bed-specific adaptation, and no held-out defence was in place.
A leakage blocklist and a held-out evaluation split are possible
protections, but neither flew in a campaign reported here. Separately, a role
read an adjudication file left readable in a run root; a round of rulings was
then re-run in isolation. Disclosure therefore does not establish uncontaminated
generalization or support claims about held-out performance.

\paragraph{Operational frictions.} Windows frictions are symmetric across
arms; one network outage was not. Judge residual risk remains after the
hardening of \S\ref{sec:measurement}: its error rate here is unmeasured,
and a seed-3 outage degraded 174 gradings to the legacy string matcher,
103 no-graph against 71 graph, the direction that flatters the graph arm;
whether any reached a counted outcome was not checked \F{69}.

\paragraph{A crash family concentrated on one arm.} The recorder introduced a
crash family --- binary content reaching the raw JSONL, killing the evolve
stage --- that produced 164 tracebacks across the three graph seeds against
5 across the three no-graph seeds, with five meta-chain holes \F{56}. These
failures occur after the task batch, so they do not change that round's
score. They can, however, remove proposals, ships, metadata or other inputs
to later rounds. Those downstream effects remain unmeasured and
arm-confounded; any retrospective association with later ship or outcome
differences would not isolate causal impact.

\paragraph{H1's component effects remain unidentified.} The whole-stack
design identifies neither the graph-evidence effect on localization
\F{9c} nor its effect on outcomes. The fixed-bundle trial found no
improvement \F{34}, but estimates that bundle's efficacy rather than
graph evidence or GHX. Its rules were written before launch and committed
with the verdict; the archaeology remains post-hoc capability evidence
\F{38}.

\paragraph{Disclosure is not mitigation.} The post-hoc scoring rule and
the efficacy trial's working-notes pre-specification are declared rather
than controlled for (\S\ref{sec:evaluator-bias}). Neither is mitigated;
both remain live limits on the intervals this thesis reports.

\paragraph{The guard event is one adjacent-round pair.} Found by a sweep,
not pre-specified, with concurrent ships and no same-window counterfactual
\F{39}\F{49}.

\paragraph{The evaluator.} The project's earlier reading of the base
loop's proposals was reversed by the archived evidence \F{38}.

\section{Cost accounting and limitations}
\label{sec:cost}
% -------------------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection*{The cost work is not a contribution of the graph}

Noop-round batching (\$38.1 to \$11.8 per round \F{13}), single-shot
digestion and the retention policy are \emph{harness-level} mechanisms:
each runs on the unmodified official system and none needs an execution
graph, so the cost account labels them \emph{portable} \F{12} and
Appendix~\ref{app:cost} describes them as engineering. The unresolved
cache-counter observation is likewise a harness-level diagnostic, not a
measured graph effect \F{56}.

\subsection*{What the graph layer costs is unknown}

On the whitelist campaigns the task-side spend per fresh evaluation is
\emph{lower} in the graph arms on all three seeds (\$0.392, \$0.472,
\$0.503 against \$0.461, \$0.538, \$0.544) \F{13}, but that comparison
settles nothing: it excludes the meta tier, which is where graph work would
appear and for which no admissible per-role accounting exists. The entry is
a blank rather than a minus, and an earlier net-expensive reading, measured
on campaigns the whitelist later excluded, is withdrawn.

\section{Future work}
\label{sec:future}
% -------------------------------------------------------------------------

Each item is attached to a measured gap. Their order is organizational,
not an empirical ranking of unresolved causes; item 1 remains the
discriminating experiment identified in \S\ref{sec:proposition}.

\paragraph{1. Readout and actual gate validity.} Attach rung R3 to the
retention decision using paired prefix replay~\cite{shepherd,car},
sibling-rollout controls~\cite{craft}, and anytime-valid sequential
tests~\cite{pace}. For the gate, preserve the recorded refusal set and
replay the actual refusals, together with suitable passed
controls, on multiple pre-specified predicted tasks. A structural trace
audit should distinguish support, graph presence and actual intervention;
blind outcome adjudication should then assess task results. The selected shipped-candidate stress test's
$6/22$ to $2/22$ change remains selection-conditional \F{71}; it does not
estimate the false-refusal probability among candidates actually refused,
and criterion error does not establish candidate efficacy.

\paragraph{2. Calibrate repeat and judge reliability.} Freeze a particular
harness configuration within each arm; within each arm, hold the task set,
model and experimental settings fixed across replicated runs. Repeat the
complete 100-task bed using full fresh evaluations, with same-instant or
interleaved repeats as motivated by \F{9e} and \F{70}. The aim is
to measure each arm's own flip and score variation and compare common,
interleaved endpoints; equal noise would still not identify any component's
efficacy. Result caching at the observed web-variance injection point --- a
web search in 75 of 78 cases \F{25} --- should be evaluated separately,
with attention to the volatile family \F{41}\F{44}\F{45}. Before new
solver runs, align the fallback log calls with task, round and final counted
rows; their counted impact is currently unknown \F{69}. Blindly
adjudicate affected outputs and a representative hardened-judge sample to
estimate false-positive and false-negative rates.

\paragraph{3. Trace budget-exhaustion changes to task outcomes.} Start with
the four archived whole-bed pairs in \F{49}. For the same task IDs, map
\texttt{budget\_exceeded}$\rightarrow$correct or wrong and the reverse,
and audit exact version, configuration and source diffs so budget or step
changes are distinguished from concurrent processor, prompt and tool edits.
Only then run fixed-configuration full-bed repeats and isolated edits or
feasible ablations, holding tasks and budget fixed except when budget is the
treatment. The guard event already records 12 of 16 newly passing tasks as
previously budget-exhausted \F{39}; neither that task-level observation nor
the net-$31$ pairs establish causal mediation or a graph benefit.

\paragraph{4. Stage component ablations and widen typed reach.} The current
whole-stack design does not isolate recording, graph evidence, verification
or typed operations \F{9c}. Because those components have cumulative
dependencies, use a staged ablation ladder and only feasible combinations,
with common readout, pre-declared endpoints and controls where interactions
require them. Report structural validity, checkability, localization and
outcome separately; no component efficacy is established here. Preserve the
reach comparison: GHX types seven processor operations, two tool-config
operations and prompt parameter mutations, while the prose Evolver can write
new processor classes and the harness source remains outside the typed
surface (\S\ref{sec:candidate-surface}). Expand the typed surface to prose
reach, then compare both under item 1's readout.

\paragraph{5. Replicate the episodic-note channel} under a pre-registered,
budget-bounded protocol \F{35}. It is solver-level and bypasses the evolve
loop, so it tests a mechanism class distinct from the property studied here
(\S\ref{sec:survivor}).

\paragraph{6. Procure capability for the wrong-closure family.} Offline
simulation over the trial repetitions lifts majority-at-$k$ mean pass from
$0.35$ to $0.60$ at $k{=}10$, entirely through scatter-shaped tasks (3
lifted, 0 sunk), while consensus-wrong tasks remain at zero for every $k$
\F{42}. Plain voting hardens the confident-wrong answer in this sample;
external discrimination is a candidate capability to test beyond plain
voting.

\paragraph{7. Measure full graph cost.} Extend accounting beyond task-side
spend to the meta tier, recording per-role, per-round and per-candidate
costs, tokens, cache behavior and retries, together with recording and
evidence work. Reconstruct only what the archive supports, and restore the
missing fields prospectively. Until then the task-side comparison of
\S\ref{sec:cost} cannot establish that the graph is cheaper or costlier.

\section{Conclusion}
% -------------------------------------------------------------------------

The loop records diagnoses, proposals, tools and abstentions, while
this study does not identify whether outcome limits arise from capability,
efficacy or retention readout. On this bed, band crossing does not isolate
edit efficacy; typed candidate operations and the readout ladder make those
questions testable. The guard event records a mechanism-consistent reduction
in starvation exits, and the processor was later dropped without an efficacy
readout; concurrent ships and the missing same-window counterfactual prevent
causal attribution.

The implemented components comprise a typed graph runtime, new
build-time and execution-time gates, and a machine-generated candidate
surface, set against a claim that stays narrow --- typed-candidate legality
and checkability, layered identity for graph-represented processor edits, and
generated-configuration and edit-ledger inspectability for registry operations
and mixed candidates rather than a wider reach. The build is enabling
infrastructure for the readout question,
not an efficient route to checkability: a lighter instrument might have
answered the same question at lower engineering cost.

\subsection*{What the thesis claims, restated}
% -------------------------------------------------------------------------

The six contributions stand as stated in \S\ref{sec:contributions}: an
independent execution and reading of the official system (C1), the
construction proof for its evidence column (C2), GHX's typed-candidate
legality and checkability without wider reach (C3), the diagnosis that the
graph-evidence component estimand was not identified, with the measurement
methodology around it (C4), the capability/efficacy separation and the
conditional readout proposition (C5), and a roadmap attached to measured
gaps (C6). Two limits travel with every
number above: the band behind every graph-arm reading is the no-graph
arm's (\S\ref{sec:threats}), and thirteen of the whitelist graph
campaign's 49 swing tasks are leak-route \F{31}; three more bound the
readings themselves: the judge's error rate here is unmeasured and 174
seed-3 gradings fell back to string matching \F{69}, and the localization
score is post hoc (\S\ref{sec:threats}). Two operational limits sit
beside them: the recorder's crash family is concentrated on the graph arm
(\S\ref{sec:threats}, \F{56}), and the meta-tier cost entry is a blank,
not a minus (\S\ref{sec:cost}).

The proposition is stated once more, with its boundary: self-evolution is
variation plus selection plus \emph{readout}, and on this bed and system
class small selected effects may be mis-ranked by the score readout,
leaving expected cumulative gain non-positive. It is a hypothesis, not an
estimated dynamic; the rival actuator account remains live
(\S\ref{sec:proposition}), and the experiment that could separate the two
is named, not claimed.
