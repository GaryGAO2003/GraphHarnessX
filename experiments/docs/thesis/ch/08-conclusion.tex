\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{ch:conclusion}
% =========================================================================

The loop can see, classify, prescribe, invent tools, and abstain --- and it
cannot hear whether any of it worked. This thesis argued the loop is deaf,
measured its floor on three seeds, showed that clearing that
floor is neither necessary nor sufficient for an edit to have worked,
retyped the candidate surface, exhibited the one ratchet click whose
mechanism the record can still read, and left the readout ladder standing
with three of its four rungs live.

% -------------------------------------------------------------------------
\section*{What the graph bought, stated without the score axis}
% -------------------------------------------------------------------------

Four things changed, and all four are checkable.

\begin{itemize}
  \item The false ``output used'' verdict reaches $86.0\%$ of no-graph
        dossiers and \textbf{0 of 859} graph-campaign dossiers
        \F{4}\F{7}.
  \item Digests carry twice the verified citation anchors at a seventh of
        the failure rate \F{52}.
  \item Candidates stop being hand-composed YAML and become typed graph
        edits, committed atomically with both artifacts machine-generated,
        halving the share that dies malformed \F{53}.
  \item Every claim a candidate makes can be replayed against the recorded
        execution --- the sixth gate's 18 refusals are 18 candidates caught
        naming a node that was never there \F{53}.
\end{itemize}

\emph{None of this made the loop smarter.} It made the loop
\textbf{checkable}: the same system, converted from one a reader must take
on trust into one a reader can audit.

On a bed whose readout is deaf, that is the honest description of the
contribution --- and it is the reason the roadmap attaches the next effort
to the readout rather than to the candidate generator.

That contribution came from a large build: a typed graph runtime, new
build-time and execution-time gates, and a machine-generated candidate
surface, set against a credited claim that stays narrow --- legality and
identity, not reach. The build is defended as enabling infrastructure for
the readout question this thesis asks, not as an efficient route to
checkability; a lighter instrument might have answered the same question
at lower engineering cost, and this thesis does not claim to have found
the cheapest one.

% -------------------------------------------------------------------------
\section*{What the thesis claims, restated}
% -------------------------------------------------------------------------

C1 is the first independent execution of the official system. C2 is the
construction proof that its evidence column cannot register the use of a
short fact, and the reach of that column into shipped decisions. C3 is GHX
--- legality and identity of candidates, explicitly not a wider reach. C4
is the pre-registered test of H1, not supported on declared substitute
endpoints, together with the methodology establishing that the registered
endpoint was unmeasurable. C5 is the separation of capability from
efficacy, and the readout proposition it supports. C6 is a roadmap in which
every item is attached to a measured gap.

The proposition itself is stated once more, with its boundary:
self-evolution is variation plus selection plus \emph{readout}, and when
the readout's resolution falls below the effect sizes being selected on,
retention degenerates into drift regardless of how good the variation is.
The rival account --- that the loop's actuator simply cannot reach the
lesions --- is not dismissed. It is weighed in \S\ref{sec:proposition},
where it explains the hard family and fails to explain the loss of the one
drug that worked or the treadmill that re-derives drugs already correctly
prescribed. The experiment that would separate them is named rather than
claimed.

By question: \textbf{Q1}, diagnosis, is answered: the evidence was false
and retention was noise-bounded. \textbf{Q2}, non-interference, is
answered: the recorder did not disturb the loop it watched. \textbf{Q3},
the effect of graph evidence, is answered: not detectable, on a
registered endpoint that was not measurable at all.

% -------------------------------------------------------------------------
\section*{The durable artifacts}
% -------------------------------------------------------------------------

Four things outlive the campaigns. The vendored substrate with its parity
and honesty gates, which lets anyone re-run the official system and know
they ran the official system. The fact ledger, in which every number in
this document has a row naming the script that recomputes it, and a
retraction registry recording every claim this project withdrew ---
including several of its own headline readings. The efficacy-trial
protocol, which is what a manual level-3 readout costs, written down so
that the automated version has a specification. And the readout ladder,
offered as a standard for a field that currently admits its candidates at
level 0 and reports nothing above it.

The measurement that mattered most was not made on the treated arm. It was
made on the instrument.
