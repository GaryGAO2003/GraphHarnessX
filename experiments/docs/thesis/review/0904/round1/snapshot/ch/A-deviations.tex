\chapter{Methodological Deviation Register}
\label{app:deviations}
% =========================================================================

Every departure from the protocol the official paper describes, with its
pre-declared fallback where one existed and its effect on claim strength.
The register exists so that a reader can reconstruct what was changed and
decide independently whether the change mattered; entries are listed even
where the effect is judged to be none.

It closes with the error registry --- the fifteen measurement and design
errors caught during this work --- because a register of what was
deliberately changed is not complete without a register of what was
accidentally got wrong.

% -------------------------------------------------------------------------
\section*{A.1\quad The three scope rulings}
% -------------------------------------------------------------------------

Three rulings narrowed what this thesis is allowed to claim. All three are
dated, and their directions are recorded because a scope ruling made after
seeing the data is only credible if its direction is stated.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{The evidence whitelist} (2026-08-26). Two graph campaigns
        flown before the graph layer was correct are excluded from every
        claim, as are all runs predating the baseline campaign. The ruling
        was made \emph{after} those campaigns had run and been read, and it
        moved the headline numbers \emph{against} the graph: the
        arm-comparison of localization lift that it invalidated was the
        project's most graph-favourable reading. Nothing measured on them
        is reported anywhere in this thesis.
  \item \textbf{The sixteen-round presentation window} (2026-08-27). All
        campaigns are read over R0--R15. The baseline campaign's
        seventeenth round falls outside that window, is used by no
        reported statistic, and is not part of the evidence base
        \F{51}. It is disclosed rather than dropped silently, because its
        \mbox{(R15, R16)} same-config window would have contributed the
        largest swing in the record: excluding it makes the reported
        noise band \emph{narrower} than the full record supports, so
        every readability verdict in this thesis stays conservative in
        the direction that makes separation easier to claim. The
        ruling is direction-neutral by construction: it was made before the
        third seed flew and applied identically to all six campaigns.
  \item \textbf{The third seed's mid-campaign bed switch} (2026-08-28). The
        seed-3 no-graph arm was moved from the 103-task bed to its 100-task
        no-pixel subset at round 7, removing a bed-size confound against
        the graph arms. Every reading in this thesis is recomputed on the
        100-task subset from \texttt{task\_history} regardless
        (\S\ref{sec:beds}), so the switch changes what was flown, not what
        is reported.
\end{itemize}

\section*{A.2\quad Deviations from the published protocol}
% -------------------------------------------------------------------------

\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.4em,style=nextline,itemsep=5pt]

  \item[\textnormal{\textbf{Paper-versus-release divergences.}}] Five
        places where the released code does not do what the paper says,
        established by reading the release (\S\ref{sec:motivation},
        \S\ref{sec:official-strengths}). \emph{(i)} The variant pool and
        ensemble routing behind the paper's GAIA headline are absent: no
        \texttt{variant}, \texttt{ensemble}, \texttt{fork} or
        \texttt{retire} path exists in any branch, the orchestrator
        describes itself as a six-stage loop, and its \texttt{num\_evolvers}
        field is annotated as no longer dispatching. \emph{(ii)} The global
        ratchet is not implemented; the release substitutes an advisory
        watchlist over adjacent rounds, the counterfactual gate, and the
        post-round rollback rule at \texttt{run\_meta\_aegis.py:901--911}
        (\S\ref{sec:open-loop}). \emph{(iii)} The $\pm 5\%$ noise threshold
        is not implemented (next item but one). \emph{(iv)} Three seeds per
        cell is the paper's table; the code defaults to $k{=}1$ and rises
        to $k{=}2$ only on a focus set. \emph{(v)} The Planner's
        per-candidate briefs are retired; it writes one landscape and a
        single Evolver session chooses how many candidates to author. The
        GAIA runner additionally defaults to a single task
        (\texttt{MAX\_TASKS\_DEFAULT=1}) and the repository's
        \texttt{.gitignore} excludes run outputs, which is why no GAIA
        number in the release is recomputable \F{19}.

  \item[\textnormal{\textbf{Model tier.}}] \begingroup\sloppy The solver is
        \texttt{deepseek-v4-flash}; the four meta roles and the judge are
        \texttt{deepseek-v4-pro}; all run through a gateway, in every
        campaign (\S\ref{sec:beds}). The paper's one
        complete experiment used a frontier model on the meta tier and a
        different bed (\S\ref{sec:beds}). \emph{Effect on claim strength:}
        none. No claim in this thesis is about model tiers, and every
        comparison is within-configuration.\par\endgroup

  \item[\textnormal{\textbf{Edit-surface coverage.}}] The typed candidate
        surface covers the processor plane, two configuration sections and
        the prompt templates. Editing the harness's own source is out of
        scope (\S\ref{sec:candidate-surface}). \emph{Effect:} the typed
        surface is strictly \emph{narrower} than the official free-text
        one, so it is conservative against every claim this thesis makes
        for the graph.

  \item[\textnormal{\textbf{The registered L5 deviation.}}] One rung of the
        switch ladder modifies official prompt content --- the only rung
        that does. It is registered because it is a named confound for any
        behavioural difference that would otherwise be attributed to
        evidence rather than to instruction.

  \item[\textnormal{\textbf{Round indexing.}}] Round~0 is a pure baseline
        round and does not enter the engine; adaptation begins at round~1,
        so $N$ configured rounds yield $N-1$ adaptation rounds. This is a
        consequence of the design in which the Digester reads the previous
        round's ledgers --- round~0 must seed them. Every window in this
        thesis is stated in the sixteen-round convention, and round~0 is
        excluded from every trend and gain-face reading
        (\S\ref{sec:trend}, \S\ref{sec:gain-face}).

  \item[\textnormal{\textbf{pass@1 throughout.}}] The paper's main
        evaluation is pass@2. Every score in this thesis is pass@1, and
        mixed-$k$ passes do not count. \emph{Effect:} it lowers scores,
        including the baseline campaign's own seed round, which moved from
        67 to 57 (\S\ref{sec:evaluator-bias}). Disclosed in the body
        precisely because that direction flatters this thesis.

  \item[\textnormal{\textbf{The $\pm 5\%$ noise threshold.}}] The paper
        tabulates an ignored single-round delta of $\pm 5\%$ without saying
        where it applies. It is adopted here as an \emph{analysis-layer}
        reading only: a single-round aggregate move inside the band carries
        no directional claim. The per-task gate is unchanged. The runner's
        own post-round rollback rule --- revert the round's ships when the
        rate falls by $5$ points \emph{and} the count by $3$ tasks
        (\texttt{run\_meta\_aegis.py:901--911}) --- is a separate,
        vendored mechanism and was left exactly as the released code has
        it.

  \item[\textnormal{\textbf{Single lineage.}}] No campaign in this thesis
        records a fork event, so the variant pool holds one variant
        throughout. The paper's routing, forking and retirement machinery
        is therefore never exercised, and no claim is made about it. Every
        result here concerns a single evolving configuration.

  \item[\textnormal{\textbf{Manifest schema and level-2 evidence.}}]
        \cls{B} The paper's change-manifest contract does not exist in the
        open repository, whose meta-agent writes journal vocabulary
        instead; paper-only fields are marked missing rather than
        fabricated. Where the meta roles author no capability evidence, the
        recipe measures the candidate's real tool output through the
        production serializer and passes or fails the gate on that
        measurement. Both are recorded in the project's
        paper-versus-reimplementation ledger, which is maintained
        separately and covers
        in addition the Ensemble machinery this thesis does not exercise.

  \item[\textnormal{\textbf{Bed asymmetry.}}] 103 tasks against the
        100-task no-pixel subset, with the third seed's no-graph arm
        switching mid-campaign. Every cross-arm reading is recomputed on
        the common subset (\S\ref{sec:beds}), so the asymmetry is a fact
        about what was flown rather than about what is reported.

  \item[\textnormal{\textbf{Replication-arm operational deviations.}}] One
        killed evolve stage and one killed batch on the second seed; a
        gateway outage with five poisoned rounds on the third; five
        meta-chain holes; three \texttt{-{}-start-round} re-flights. All
        are disclosed in the body rather than here
        (\S\ref{sec:replication}), with the isolation and wash procedure
        each one received.

  \item[\textnormal{\textbf{The efficacy trial's \texttt{-{}-max-steps}
        correction.}}] Registry error~15 below: the loader default of 40
        doubles the campaign step budget and revives burnout tasks. The
        trial arms were re-flown under the campaign cap.

\end{description}

\section*{A.3\quad The error registry}
% -------------------------------------------------------------------------

Fifteen errors of measurement and design were caught during this work, each
followed by a forced re-check. The registry is reported as a result
(\S\ref{sec:evaluator-bias}, \S\ref{sec:threats}): a study whose evaluator
is not itself audited has no grounds for the intervals it reports.

\paragraph{Direction.} Of the first thirteen, eight moved in the
project's own favour (1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 13), three against it (the
second reversal in 9, and 10, 12), and two were neutral (5, 11). Errors 14
and 15 were logged after that tally was struck and are not classified in
it. \emph{Both directions are represented, which is the point}: the common
cause is not motivated reasoning but \textbf{stopping the check too early}.
Errors 13, 14 and 15 were caught by the supervisor rather than the author,
each time by a question of the form ``are you sure that is not a setup
problem''.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.2em]

  \item \textbf{``88--90\% false negatives''}, quoted from a code comment.
        The figure came from an earlier campaign and had never been
        recomputed. \emph{Rule:} a ledger number must be recomputable; a
        comment is not a source.

  \item \textbf{Measuring the flow column's heuristic on the graph arm},
        where that column has already been removed --- a measurement of a
        counterfactual. \emph{Rule:} measure a signal on the arm where it
        is alive (\F{1}).

  \item \textbf{``1441/1441 dossiers carry the false signal''}, which
        counted a section heading that is printed unconditionally. The
        actual figure is 1239 of 1441, $86.0\%$ \F{4}. \emph{Rule:} a
        heading is not a trigger; count entries, not headers.

  \item \textbf{``78\% of consumed results are graded NO''}, taken with our
        own instrument as ground truth --- circular. Replaced by an
        instrument-free criterion: the answer appears verbatim in the tool
        result \emph{and} the task passed \F{2}.

  \item \textbf{A verification script returning $0.0\%$ disagreement},
        because it read a field from the wrong object and got \texttt{None}
        every time. \emph{Rule:} suspect the wiring before the hypothesis
        when a result is too clean.

  \item \textbf{``The loop doubled down on a false premise''} --- no such
        citation exists in the documents. \emph{Rule:} narrative may not be
        written before its evidence.

  \item \textbf{``76.6\% verifiable false negatives''}, using shared word
        stems, which common words such as \emph{california} and
        \emph{complete} contaminate. Tightened to answer-containment,
        giving $75.0\%$.

  \item \textbf{``A deep evidence defect only the graph can see.''} It is a
        construction mismatch between a 20-character minimum overlap and a
        median answer length of 9. \emph{Rule:} chase the mechanism to the
        bottom; ``only our system finds it'' is a separate claim needing
        separate proof.

  \item \textbf{Two reversals on the replay gate.} First the numbers were
        read as ``7 of 14 refused''; then a grep for
        \emph{let through, not verified} reversed it to ``0 refused, all
        abstained''; then reading the files whole revealed that the phrase
        is boilerplate printed in every file, and the actual verdicts are 7
        refusals and 8 abstentions on the post-fix graph campaign \F{11}. \emph{Rule:}
        neither direction of grep is reading; adjudication requires whole
        files, and template text is grep's natural trap.

  \item \textbf{``A harness-fixable ceiling of 3 tasks''}, computed on the
        never-passed queue alone. The reachable union is 97 of 100 with 47
        unstably reachable \F{16}. \emph{Rule:} a queue-scoped number is
        not a global ceiling. This error had been supporting the negative
        result.

  \item \textbf{``Same-config pair swing of $-12$''}, which mixed a $k{=}2$
        round~0 with $k{=}1$ rounds after it. Under a uniform pass@1
        reading it is $-6$ \F{15}. \emph{Rule:} lock the per-task reading
        for the whole window.

  \item \textbf{``Flow-column NO rate 68.1\%''} from a 150-call sample; 300
        calls gave $71.9\%$ and the whole arm $75.0\%$ --- a sampling drift
        of 7 points. \emph{Rule:} a ledger number is recomputed over the
        whole population or carries an interval; a convenience sample is a
        probe.

  \item \textbf{Pre-registration criteria left inside the subject's
        reachable filesystem.} The roles listed the parent directory, read
        the criteria file, and the resulting landscape quoted it verbatim
        as ``the framework for everything below''. The entire round's
        adjudication was voided and re-run in isolation. \emph{Rule:} the
        answer key does not go anywhere the subject can reach; the roles
        read everything reachable, which is the same property as copying a
        false evidence column.

  \item \textbf{``0/10 versus 0/10'' on a drug trial.} A concurrency spike
        at launch killed 8 of 20 repetitions within a second with zero
        model messages, and the runner swallowed the exception as
        \texttt{passed:\ False} without persisting a reason; two further
        phantom runs exited \texttt{done} in two seconds with no messages.
        Serial re-running gave $1/11$ against $0/12$ --- not merely
        asymmetric but conclusion-changing, since the parent arm's single
        pass was the lottery channel the drug had closed. \emph{Rule:}
        count genuine completions before reporting a score, and never let a
        swallowed exception reach the ledger as a failure.

  \item \textbf{Three ``unusually hot'' control arms} in drug trials. The
        trials had not reproduced the campaign's \texttt{-{}-max-steps 20};
        tasks fell through to the loader default of 40, and burnout-type
        tasks revive naturally at double the step budget. The fingerprints
        were the nudge text (``before step 40'' against ``before step
        20'') and the step distribution. Comparisons \emph{between} the
        trial's own arms remain internally valid, since both arms shared
        the cap; what failed was comparability with historical base rates.
        \emph{Rule:} a trial must reproduce the campaign's task parameters
        item by item, and an unexpectedly hot control arm is a parameter
        question before it is a luck question.

\end{enumerate}
